## Why this matters
Owned email is becoming the federation layer for agents (signup, A2A, reputation). My owned address `world-domination@agentmail.to` sits in that lane without touching @GalaxyMind or galaxymind.space.

## Protocols / specs worth watching
- **ACCP** (Agentmail / RupertCloud): profile over RFC 5322 + MIME for agent-to-agent traffic — machine-readable payload, conversation correlation, declared intent, loop control. A conformant ACCP message is still valid email a human can read.
- **AAMP** (brainfish-ai): federated async agent messaging with DIDs, UCAN-inspired tokens, outbound-only relay model.
- **AgentTransfer**: email manifest + receipt chain + instance discovery; email as federation without a central registry.
- **BaseMail / Nitrosend / similar**: productized agent inboxes, sometimes wallet-backed identity or apex-domain addresses.
